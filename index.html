Hailing from Bandung, 510 is a rock band built on the musical foundations of Slap It Out. Established in 2021, the group delivers a powerful sonic experience driven by heavy rock textures and intensely personal, straightforward songwriting.

This website is not an official 510 website. It is a personal archive where a place to collect the music, visuals, and concert memories that made following the band worth remembering.
